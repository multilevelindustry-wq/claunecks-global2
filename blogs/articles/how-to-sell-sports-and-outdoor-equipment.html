Frequently Asked Questions

What should I prepare before publishing?

Prepare an accurate title, a complete description, relevant images, key specifications, availability information, and a clear way for interested people to contact you. The exact details depend on the offer, but accuracy and clarity matter in every category.

How can I make my page more useful to search visitors?

Answer the main question near the beginning, use descriptive headings, include related terms naturally, add useful original details, and keep the page updated. Avoid keyword stuffing and do not create claims that the offer cannot support.

Does Claunecks Global guarantee sales, downloads, enquiries, or employment?

No. A marketplace listing can improve discoverability, but results depend on the quality and relevance of the offer, demand, pricing, accuracy, competition, and the response provided by the publisher. No particular result is guaranteed.
